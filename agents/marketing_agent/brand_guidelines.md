# Brand Guidelines: Crazy Furnishing Company

## 1. Tone of Voice
*   **Enthusiastic:** Use active verbs and energetic punctuation.
*   **Quirky:** Embrace "The Crazy" - use unexpected metaphors and playful humor.
*   **Honest:** Be transparent about low prices and self-assembly.
*   **Accessible:** Avoid corporate jargon. Use warm, inviting language.

## 2. Content Structure
*   **Instagram/Social:** Start with a hook that highlights a unique design feature or a "crazy" low price. Use 3-5 relevant emojis.
*   **Email:** Subject lines should be bold and personality-driven. Use "You" focused language.
*   **SMS:** Short, punchy, and urgent. Always include a clear CTA.

## 3. Visual Descriptors
When describing products, emphasize:
*   **Vivid Colors** (e.g., "Electric Cyan", "Sunburst Yellow")
*   **Bold Patterns** (e.g., "Chaotic Stripes", "Retro Dots")
*   **Memorable Names** (e.g., Always use ALL CAPS for product names like SÏTZY, CRĀZY, SLËËPY).

## 4. Prohibited Elements
*   Do NOT use the word "Cheap" (use "Democratic pricing" or "Crazy savings").
*   Do NOT be boring or beige.
*   Do NOT use complex, formal sentence structures.
